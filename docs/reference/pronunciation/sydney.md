# Sydney (KIIS 90s SYD) Pronunciation Guide

This document covers Greater Sydney place names that a generic text-to-speech engine typically mispronunciations, with locally-correct phonetic respellings and plain-ASCII alias rules for the ElevenLabs eleven_v3 pronunciation dictionary. Each entry replaces only the proper-noun token. An operator MUST audition every respelling in the ElevenLabs studio before going to air.

## How to read this

Alias respellings only (v3 ignores phoneme); each replaces only the proper-noun token; audition before air. Stress is marked with CAPS in the Correct column.

| Warringah | wa-RING-ah (stress on 2nd syllable) | WAH-ring-ah | Wa-ring-gah | Aboriginal origin: "rain" or "plenty of rain". Stress on first syllable. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warringah,_New_South_Wales |
| Coogee | KOO-gee (short "oo" as in "book") | KOO-jee | Koo-jee | Aboriginal origin: "stinking place". Final consonant is "j" not "g". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coogee,_New_South_Wales |
| Kirribilli | KIR-ri-bil-lee (stress on 1st) | ki-ruh-BI-lee | Kirra-billy | Aboriginal origin: "good viewpoint". Stress on third syllable; double-l makes a long "lee" sound. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirribilli,_New_South_Wales |
| Macquarie | mak-WAH-ree (stress on 2nd) | muh-KWOR-ee | Ma-kwor-ee | Named after Governor Macquarie. First vowel is a schwa, stress on second syllable. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macquarie_Street,_Sydney |
| Cronulla | kru-HUH-nuh-luh (extra syllable) | kroh-NULL-ah | Kro-null-ah | Aboriginal origin: "red glowing sunset". First syllable is "kroh" not "kruh"; three syllables not four. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cronulla,_New_South_Wales |
| Pyrmont | PEER-mont (rhymes with "beer") | PIR-mont | Pir-mont | Named after a promontory in Devon. First syllable has a short "i" as in "pirate", not "peer". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrmont,_New_South_Wales |
| Glebe | gleb (rhymes with "ebb") | GLEEB | Gleeb | Named after a church landholding. One syllable with a long "ee" sound. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glebe,_New_South_Wales |
| Balmain | bal-MAN (stress on 2nd) | BAL-min | Bal-min | Named after William Balmain. Stress on first syllable; final syllable reduces to a schwa. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balmain,_New_South_Wales |
| Goulburn | GOO-burn (rhymes with "cool") | GULL-burn | Gull-burn | Named after Henry Goulburn. First syllable is "gull" not "goo". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goulburn,_New_South_Wales |
| Botany | BOH-tuh-nee (long "o") | BOT-uh-nee | Bot-uh-nee | Aboriginal origin (Bidjigal). First syllable has a short "o" as in "bot", not a long "oh". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany,_New_South_Wales |
| Kogarah | KO-gah-rah (even stress) | kuh-GARR-ah | Kuh-gar-rah | Aboriginal origin: "red earth". Stress on second syllable; the "garr" rhymes with "car". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kogarah,_New_South_Wales |
| Penshurst | PENS-hurst (stress on 1st) | PENZ-hurst | Penz-hurst | The "s" before "h" is voiced as "z". TTS may say "s" not "z". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penshurst,_New_South_Wales |
| Narrabeen | NARR-ah-been (even "a") | nah-RAH-been | Nah-ra-been | Aboriginal origin. Stress on second syllable; first vowel is a schwa. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narrabeen,_New_South_Wales |
| Mosman | MOS-man (short "o" as in "moss") | MOHZ-man | Moze-man | First syllable has a long "o" sound. Locals say "MOHZ-man". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosman,_New_South_Wales |
| Ryde | RID (rhymes with "kid") | RIDE | Ride | Named after a ridge. Pronounced exactly like the verb "ride" with a long "i". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryde,_New_South_Wales |
| Drummoyne | DRUM-oy-nuh (3 syllables) | drum-OYN | Drum-oin | Named after a Scottish estate. Two syllables: "drum" + "OYN" (rhymes with "coin"). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drummoyne,_New_South_Wales |
| Cabramatta | ka-bra-MAT-uh (stress on 2nd) | kab-ruh-MAHT-uh | Kab-ra-maht-uh | Aboriginal origin: "head of the creek". Stress on third syllable. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabramatta,_New_South_Wales |
| Campbelltown | CAM-belt-town (even stress) | CAM-bull-tun | Cam-bull-tun | Named after a convict. Second syllable is a schwa, not a full "bell". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campbelltown,_New_South_Wales |
| Tahmoor | TAY-moor (rhymes with "door") | TAY-muh | Tay-muh | Aboriginal origin. Final syllable is a schwa, not "moor". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahmoor,_New_South_Wales |
| Ku-ring-gai | KOO-ring-guy (even stress) | kuh-RING-eye | Kuh-ring-eye | Aboriginal origin: "people from the sandstone country". Stress on second syllable. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ku-ring-gai,_New_South_Wales |
| Jannali | JAN-uh-lee (stress on 1st) | juh-NAIL-ee | Juh-nail-ee | Aboriginal origin: "the moon". Stress on second syllable; "nali" rhymes with "nail-y". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jannali,_New_South_Wales |
