# Darwin (Hot 100) Pronunciation Guide

Pronunciations for Darwin & Palmerston (NT) place names that trip up generic TTS. These feed the Hot 100 traffic bulletin's ElevenLabs eleven_v3 alias pronunciation dictionary. An operator MUST audition each respelling in the ElevenLabs studio before it goes to air.

## How to read this
Alias respellings only (v3 ignores phoneme); each replaces only the proper-noun token; audition before air.

## Suburbs & localities

| Name | Avoid (common / TTS mispronunciation) | Correct (plain phonetic, e.g. "muh-NASH") | Alias respelling | Notes & source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parap | "pa-RAHP" | PA-rap | Pa-rap | From "Paraparap" (pastoral property); stress on first syllable. NT Place Names Register & Wikipedia confirm origin. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parap,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parap,_Northern_Territory) |
| Berrimah | "behr-EE-mah" or "berr-EYE-mah" | BERR-ih-mah | Berry-mah | Aboriginal word meaning "to the south"; stress on first syllable. Named by military before WWII. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berrimah,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berrimah,_Northern_Territory) |
| Woolner | "WOOL-ner" (like "wool") | WUL-ner | Wool-ner | Named after the Woolner (Wulna) Aboriginal tribal group; the "oo" is short, not the long "oo" of "wool". Locals say "wul" to rhyme with "full". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woolner,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woolner,_Northern_Territory) |
| Anula | "AN-yoo-luh" or "AY-nuh-lah" | ah-NOO-luh | Ah-noola | Named after the Anula group of the Yanyuwa nation from the Gulf of Carpentaria. Stress on second syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anula,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anula,_Northern_Territory) |
| Malak | "mal-AK" | MAH-lak | Mah-lak | Named after an Aboriginal tribe from the Daly River area; intended pronunciation was "Mulluck". Stress on first syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malak,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malak,_Northern_Territory) |
| Coonawarra | "koon-ah-WAR-uh" (stressed final syllable) | koo-nah-WAR-uh | Koo-na-warra | Aboriginal word meaning "swan"; from HMAS Coonawarra naval establishment. Stress on third syllable, not fourth. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coonawarra,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coonawarra,_Northern_Territory) |
| Ludmilla | "lud-MILL-uh" or "LOOD-mill-uh" | lood-MIL-uh | Lood-milla | Named after Ludmilla Holtze, a German immigrant who arrived in Darwin in 1872. Stress on second syllable; first syllable is "lood" not "lud". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludmilla,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludmilla,_Northern_Territory) |
| Wulagi | "WOO-lah-gee" or "woo-LAH-jee" | woo-LAH-gee | Wool-ah-gee | Aboriginal name; stress on second syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wulagi,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wulagi,_Northern_Territory) |
| Karama | "kah-RAH-mah" or "KARE-ah-mah" | KAH-rah-mah | Kah-rah-mah | Aboriginal name; stress on first syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karama,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karama,_Northern_Territory) |
| Winnellie | "win-EL-lee" or "win-EL-eye" | WIN-ell-ee | Winn-ell-ee | Aboriginal name; stress on first syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnellie,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnellie,_Northern_Territory) |
| Nightcliff | "NITE-cliff" (rhymes with "night cliff") | NITE-kliff | Nite-kliff | Named from HMS Beagle crew landing at a cliffy projection after dark in 1839. Locals say as two English words "night cliff" compressed. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nightcliff,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nightcliff,_Northern_Territory) |
| Bagot | "BAY-got" or "bah-GOH" | BAG-ut | Bag-ut | Named after Charles Boydell Bagot; stress on first syllable, "a" as in "cat". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagot,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagot,_Northern_Territory) |
| Casuarina | "kah-swah-REE-nuh" or "kass-yoo-AH-ree-nuh" | kaz-yoo-ah-REE-nuh | Kaz-yoo-ah-ree-na | From Malay "kasuari" (cassowary); four syllables, stress on third. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casuarina,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casuarina,_Northern_Territory) |
| Brinkin | "BRINK-in" or "BRING-kin" | BRIN-kin | Brin-kin | Named after the Brinkin (Marrithiyal) Aboriginal people south of Daly River mouth. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brinkin,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brinkin,_Northern_Territory) |
| Humpty Doo | "HUM-tee DOO" or "HUM-pee DOO" | HUM-pee DOO | Hum-pee-doo | From "Umpity Doo" (1910 survey plan); locals say "Humpty" as in the nursery rhyme character, not "Hump-tee". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humpty_Doo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humpty_Doo) |

## Roads, freeways & highways

| Name | Avoid | Correct | Alias respelling | Notes & source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stuart Highway | "STOO-urt" or "STYOO-urt" | STOO-urt | Stoo-urt | Named after explorer John McDouall Stuart; locals say "STOO-urt" with a long "oo" as in "boot". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_Highway) |
| Tiger Brennan Drive | "TY-ger BREN-nan" | TY-ger BREN-nan | Tiger-brennan | Named after Thomas "Tiger" Brennan; usually read correctly but TTS may stress "Tiger" oddly. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Brennan_Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Brennan_Drive) |
| McMillans Road | "mak-MILL-anz" or "muh-SMILL-anz" | mik-MIL-uns | Mik-mill-uns | Named after early Darwin settler; stress on second syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMillans_Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMillans_Road) |
| Roystonea Avenue | "roy-STOH-nuh" or "roy-STOH-nee-uh" | roy-STO-nee-a | Roy-stone-ee-a | Named after the Roystonea palm genus; four syllables, stress on second. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roystonea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roystonea) |
| Berrimah Road | "behr-EE-mah" | BERR-ih-mah | Berry-mah | Aboriginal word meaning "to the south"; stress on first syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berrimah,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berrimah,_Northern_Territory) |
| Trower Road | "TROH-ver" (like "trough") | TROW-er | Trow-er | Locals say "TROW-er" rhyming with "cow-er", not "TROH-ver". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trower_Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trower_Road) |
| Vanderlin Drive | "van-DER-lin" | VAN-der-lin | Van-der-lin | Named after explorer; stress on first syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderlin_Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderlin_Drive) |
| Amy Johnson Avenue | "AY-mee JON-son" | AY-mee JON-son | Amy-johnson | Named after aviator Amy Johnson; TTS may stress "Johnson" oddly. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Johnson) |
| Dick Ward Drive | "DICK ward" (flattened) | DICK Ward | Dick-ward | Named after Dick Ward; two distinct syllables, "Ward" stressed. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Ward_Drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Ward_Drive) |

## Bridges, tunnels & landmarks

| Name | Avoid | Correct | Alias respelling | Notes & source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cullen Bay | "KULL-en" or "KOO-len" | KULL-en | Cullen-bay | Named after an early colonial figure; "Cullen" rhymes with "dull" + "en". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cullen_Bay,_Darwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cullen_Bay,_Darwin) |
| East Point | "EEST" or stressed wrongly | EAST Point | East-point | Locals say as two clear English words "East Point". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Point,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Point,_Northern_Territory) |
| Fannie Bay | "FAN-eye" or "FAN-ee" | FAN-ee | Fannie-bay | Named after a person; "Fannie" rhymes with "Danny". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fannie_Bay,_Northern_Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fannie_Bay,_Northern_Territory) |
| Darwin Waterfront | "WAR-ter-front" | WAW-ter-front | Waterfront | Standard Australian pronunciation; "wa" as in "water" not "war". [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darwin_Waterfront_Precinct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darwin_Waterfront_Precinct) |
| Kormilda College | "kor-MIL-dah" or "kor-MILL-dah" | kor-MIL-da | Kor-milda | Named after an Aboriginal word; stress on second syllable. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kormilda_College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kormilda_College) |
