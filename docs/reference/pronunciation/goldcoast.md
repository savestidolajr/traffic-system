# Gold Coast (Hot Tomato 102.9) Pronunciation Guide

Locally-correct pronunciations for Gold Coast suburbs, roads and landmarks used in Hot Tomato 102.9 ARN traffic bulletins. These alias respellings feed the market's eleven_v3 pronunciation dictionary — each replaces only the proper-noun token. An operator must audition every respelling in the ElevenLabs studio before air.

## How to read this

Alias respellings only (v3 ignores phoneme/IPA); each replaces only the proper-noun token; audition before air.

| Pimpama | "PIM-pah-muh" (flat stress) or "pim-PAY-muh" | PIM-pah-muh | Pim-pah-muh | Aboriginal: Bundjalung "pim pim ba" (place of soldier birds). Stress on first syllable. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pimpama,_Queensland |
| Currumbin | "KUR-rum-bin" or "kur-UM-bin" | kuh-RUM-bin | Kuh-rum-bin | Aboriginal Yugambeh: likely "cur-UM-bin"; stress on second syllable, schwa first. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currumbin,_Queensland |
| Coolangatta | "COOL-ang-at-uh" or "COO-lan-GAT-uh" | coo-lan-GAT-uh | Coo-lan-gat-uh | Named after schooner wrecked 1846. Locals say 4 syllables with stress on third. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coolangatta |
| Tallebudgera | "TAL-uh-BUD-jer-uh" or "tal-eh-boo-JER-uh" | tal-uh-BUD-juh-ruh | Tal-uh-bud-jah-ruh | Aboriginal Yugambeh; 5 syllables, stress on third. TTS often flattens to 4. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallebudgera,_Queensland |
| Numinbah | "NOO-min-bah" or "NUM-in-bah" | noo-MIN-bah | Nuu-min-bah | Aboriginal Yugambeh: "noo" not "nuh"; stress on second syllable. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numinbah_Valley |
| Coomera | "COO-mer-uh" or "kuh-MER-uh" | coo-MER-uh | Coo-mer-uh | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on second syllable, "coo" not "kuh". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coomera,_Queensland |
| Kirra | "KUR-ruh" or "KEER-uh" | KIRR-uh | Kir-ruh | Aboriginal Yugambeh: possibly "white cockatoo" or "fire"; short vowel. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coolangatta |
| Bilinga | "BIL-in-guh" or "bee-LING-uh" | bih-LING-uh | Bih-ling-uh | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on second syllable. TTS may stress first. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilinga,_Queensland |
| Clagiraba | "kla-JIR-uh-buh" or "KLAG-ih-rah-buh" | klah-JIR-uh-buh | Klah-jir-uh-buh | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on second syllable. TTS often stresses first. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clagiraba,_Queensland |
| Worongary | "WOR-on-gair-ee" or "woh-RON-gair-ee" | wo-RONG-uh-ree | Wo-rong-uh-ree | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on second syllable, "wrong" not "wore". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worongary |
| Ormeau | "OR-mo" or "or-MEE-oh" | or-MOH | Or-moh | French-derived; stress on second syllable, "oh" not "ohh". TTS treats it as English. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ormeau,_Queensland |
| Coombabah | "COOM-bah-bah" or "koom-BAB-uh" | koom-BAB-uh | Koom-bab-uh | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on second syllable. TTS stresses first. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coombabah |
| Wongawallan | "wong-uh-WAL-an" or "WON-gah-wal-an" | wong-ah-WAL-an | Wong-ah-wal-an | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on third syllable. TTS typically stresses first. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wongawallan,_Queensland |
| Elanora | "el-AN-or-uh" or "el-uh-NOR-uh" | eh-lan-OR-uh | Eh-lan-or-uh | Aboriginal: "home by the sea"; stress on third syllable. TTS may stress second. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elanora,_Queensland |
| Tugun | "TOO-gun" or "tuh-GUN" | TOO-gun | Too-gun | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on first syllable. TTS often says "tuh-GUN". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tugun,_Queensland |
| Benowa | "ben-OH-uh" or "BEN-oh-uh" | beh-NOH-uh | Beh-noh-uh | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on second syllable, "beh" not "ben". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benowa,_Queensland |
| Merrimac | "merr-IM-ak" or "muh-RIM-ak" | MEH-rih-mak | Meh-rih-mak | English-derived (Merrimack river); stress on first syllable. TTS stresses second. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrimac,_Queensland |
| Yatala | "yat-AL-uh" or "YAT-uh-luh" | yat-AH-luh | Yat-ah-luh | Aboriginal Yugambeh; stress on second syllable. TTS often stresses first. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yatala,_Queensland |
