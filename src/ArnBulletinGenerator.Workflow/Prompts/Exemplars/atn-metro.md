# ATN metro exemplars

Body copy from the 21 ATN reads in `docs/reference/atn-bulletin-style-samples.md`, with the
production wrapper removed: sponsor and hotline labels, station idents, presenter openers and
sign-offs, branded weather prefixes and driver-advice lines. The composer writes the BODY only —
the station's intro and sign-off are added around it — so an exemplar that carries a wrapper
demonstrates output the composer is forbidden to produce. `words` is the body word count and
drives exemplar selection, so it must match the text.

Three reads fused the presenter opener with the first traffic fact (`kiis1011-0829`,
`kiis1065-0739`, `kiis1065-0749`); those leads were re-cut to start at the traffic clause. Every
other word is verbatim ATN. The corpus doc stays untouched as provenance.
